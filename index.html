The Studio

About Us

“

If I designed your brand, this is where we'd start.

If I Designed was created after seeing too many brands invest in content, logos, and trends without ever building a clear foundation. Most businesses don't struggle because they lack good visuals — they struggle because they don't know what makes them different, who they're speaking to, or how they should show up consistently.

The studio was built to change that. By combining strategy, creative direction, campaigns, and social media, every decision starts with purpose, not guesswork.

Working with brands across different industries revealed the same pattern: businesses often jumped straight into content creation before defining their positioning. The strongest results always came from slowing down, understanding the brand first, and building a strategy that could guide every future decision.

That's why every project begins with clarity, because great branding isn't about looking good for a moment, it's about creating something people remember.

Meet the Founder

Maria Cruz

I'm Maria Cruz, founder of If I Designed — a creative studio specialising in brand strategy, creative direction and social media.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy and aesthetics, helping brands define who they are, how they're perceived, and how they show up consistently across every touchpoint.

I believe the strongest brands aren't built through trends or random content. They're built through clear positioning, intentional creative decisions and a deep understanding of the people they're trying to reach.

Today, I work with founders and growing brands to create identities, campaigns and content systems that feel memorable, distinctive and impossible to ignore.

Alongside running the studio, I'm completing a degree in Marketing & Communications at Universidade Católica Portuguesa, specialising in Marketing, Communication and Public Relations.
